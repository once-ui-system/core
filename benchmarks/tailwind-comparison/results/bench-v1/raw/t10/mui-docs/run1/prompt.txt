Task: Haven, a home search site for Lisbon. At `/`, a search page: a filter bar (type, price range, minimum bedrooms, and a sort by newest, price or size), a results count, and the results as cards (photo placeholder drawn with CSS, price, title, beds, baths, area, district, a badge for homes listed in the last 7 days, and a save toggle) beside an interactive map. The map uses `maplibre-gl` (installed, as is `react-map-gl`) with the free map style URL in the data, centred on the given point: this style URL and its tiles are the only network resources the app may load. Every home shows on the map as a price marker; hovering a card highlights its marker and hovering a marker highlights its card; clicking a marker opens a small popup card. Filters update the list, the count and the markers together. On phones, the list and the map are two views with a toggle. At `/homes/h-104` (and every home's own `/homes/[id]`), a detail page: a gallery strip of CSS placeholders, price and key facts, the features, an energy rating, a small map with the home's location, and a contact form for the agent (name, email, message, all required, with inline errors) beside the facts on desktop.
