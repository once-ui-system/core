---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
summary: "A notes app built so your whole team can find what it wrote."
publishedAt: "2026-10-01"
tag: "Launch"
---

Acme Notes is a notes app for teams. Notebooks are shared, so a note written by one person is there for everyone who needs it, and each organization decides who can read and who can edit.

Finding things is the point. Search runs across every notebook you can open, so the answer is a few keystrokes away instead of buried in someone's folder. Start with the Free plan for up to three members, and move to Team when you grow.
