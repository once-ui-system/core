---
title: Introducing Acme Notes
date: 2026-10-01
summary: Acme Notes is a notes app built so that your whole team can find what it wrote.
---

Acme Notes gives every team shared notebooks that stay organised as they grow. Write once, and everyone with access sees the same page.

Search is instant and roles keep access tidy: owners, admins and members each see what they should. Start on the free plan and move to Team when you are ready.
