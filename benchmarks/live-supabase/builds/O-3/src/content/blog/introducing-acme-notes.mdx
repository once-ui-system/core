---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: 2026-10-01
summary: "Meet Acme Notes, the notes app that helps your whole team find what it knows."
---

Teams write down a lot, and most of it gets lost. Acme Notes gives every team shared notebooks, so what one person learns is there for everyone else.

Search is instant, and roles and permissions keep each notebook in the right hands. Start free for up to three members, and move to the Team plan when you grow.
