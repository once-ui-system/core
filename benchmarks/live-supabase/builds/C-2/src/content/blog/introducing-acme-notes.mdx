---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: "2026-10-01"
summary: "Acme Notes is a notes app built so your whole team can find what it wrote."
---

Teams write a lot down, and most of it gets lost. Acme Notes gives every team shared notebooks, so notes live in one place instead of in personal folders.

Search is instant and roles keep the right people in control. Create an organization, invite your team by link, and start writing.
