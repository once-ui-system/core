---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
summary: "Acme Notes is a notes app that helps teams write things down and find them again."
publishedAt: "2026-10-01"
---

Teams write a lot down and lose most of it. Acme Notes keeps your notes in shared notebooks, so what one person learns is there for everyone else.

Search is instant and access is simple: owners, admins and members each get the permissions they need. Start free with up to three members, and move to Team when you grow.
