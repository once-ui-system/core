## Write your first post

Posts live in `src/content/blog` as MDX files. The frontmatter sets the title, summary, date, tag and cover image; the body is Markdown with headings, lists, links and code.

## What to write about

- Product updates and new features
- Guides that help customers get more out of your product
- Stories from your team
