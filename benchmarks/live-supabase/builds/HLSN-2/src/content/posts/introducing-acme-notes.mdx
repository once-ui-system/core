---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
summary: "Acme Notes is a notes app for teams, with shared notebooks you can search."
publishedAt: "2026-10-01"
---

Acme Notes gives a team one place for the notes it writes. Each organization has shared notebooks that its members can open, and every note can be found again with a few words from its title or body.

Organizations have owners, admins and members. Owners and admins invite people by link, and every member sees only what their role allows. Start free with up to three members, and move to the Team plan when your team grows.
