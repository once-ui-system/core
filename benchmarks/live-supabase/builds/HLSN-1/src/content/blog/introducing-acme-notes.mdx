---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
summary: "A notes app built so your whole team can find what they wrote."
publishedAt: "2026-10-01"
---

Acme Notes gives a team one place for its notes. Notes live in shared notebooks, so the people who need them can open them without asking who has the file. Each organization has its own notebooks, members and roles, and nothing one organization writes is visible to another.

Search is built in and works as you type. Find a meeting note from last quarter or a runbook someone wrote last week without remembering which notebook it went into. Owners and admins can invite people with a link that expires after seven days, and choose whether each person joins as a member or an admin.
