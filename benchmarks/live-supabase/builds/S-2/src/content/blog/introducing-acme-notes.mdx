---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
summary: "Acme Notes is a notes app that helps teams write things down and find them again."
publishedAt: "2026-10-01"
---

Teams lose a surprising amount of knowledge to scattered documents and private notes. Acme Notes puts it in shared notebooks that everyone on the team can read, write and search.

Every organization decides who can do what, with owners, admins and members. Start on the free plan with up to three members, and move to the Team plan when your team grows.
