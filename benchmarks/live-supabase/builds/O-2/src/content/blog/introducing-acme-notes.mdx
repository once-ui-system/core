---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: 2026-10-01
summary: "Acme Notes is a notes app that lets your whole team write, share and find notes in one place."
---

Today we are opening Acme Notes to teams. It gives every team shared notebooks, so ideas, meeting notes and decisions live in one place instead of scattered across personal files.

Finding things is just as easy: search is instant across every notebook, and roles and permissions let owners, admins and members decide who can read, edit and invite.
