Acme Notes is a notes app for teams. Notebooks are shared by the whole team, so the decision you made last week, the onboarding checklist and the meeting summary are all in the same place, and nobody has to ask where they went.

Search is instant. Type a few letters and you see matching notes across every notebook you can open, with the roles you hold deciding what you are allowed to read or change. Start with the free plan for up to three members, and move to the Team plan when more people join.
