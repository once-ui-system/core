---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: "2026-10-01"
summary: "A notes app that makes the team's writing easy to share and easy to find."
---

Acme Notes is a place for your team to write things down together. Notes live in shared notebooks, so the people who need a page can open it without asking for a copy, and changes show up for everyone in the same place.

Finding things is the part we cared about most. Search runs as you type across every notebook you can see, and roles decide who can invite people, rename a workspace or change the plan, so each team keeps its notes to itself.
