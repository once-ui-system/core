---
title: Introducing Acme Notes
date: "2026-10-01"
summary: Acme Notes gives every team one place for shared notebooks, fast search and clear permissions.
---

Acme Notes is a notes app built for teams. Each organization gets its own space, shared notebooks that everyone can read, and a search box that finds a note by any word in it, as you type.

We built it because team knowledge tends to end up scattered across chats, documents and private drafts. With Acme Notes, the notes live together, the owners decide who can see and change them, and new members can find what they need without asking around.
