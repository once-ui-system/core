Today we are launching Acme Notes, a notes app built for teams. Shared notebooks keep everyone's writing in one place, so nothing lives in a single person's drafts.

Finding things is just as important as writing them. Instant search looks across every notebook you can access, and roles and permissions keep each note in front of the right people.
