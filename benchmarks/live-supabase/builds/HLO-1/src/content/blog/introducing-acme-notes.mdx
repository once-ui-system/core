---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: "2026-10-01"
summary: "Acme Notes gives your team one place for shared notebooks that everyone can search."
---

Acme Notes is a notes app built for teams. Every notebook belongs to an organization, so your whole team writes in the same place and nothing gets lost in someone's personal folder.

Search runs as you type across every notebook you can see. Owners and admins decide who can edit, invite and manage the workspace, so each person sees exactly what they should.
