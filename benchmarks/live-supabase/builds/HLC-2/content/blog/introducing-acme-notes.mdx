---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: "2026-10-01"
summary: "A notes app for teams, with shared notebooks, instant search and clear roles."
---

Acme Notes gives your team one place for the notes that matter: meeting minutes, project plans, runbooks and the decisions behind them. Notes live in shared notebooks, so everyone in the organization works from the same pages instead of scattered copies.

Finding things is the point. Search answers as you type, across every notebook your role lets you see, and owners, admins and members each get exactly the access their role needs.
