---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
summary: "Acme Notes gives your team one place for shared notebooks that anyone on the team can find."
publishedAt: "2026-10-01"
---

Acme Notes is a notes app for teams. Your notes live in shared notebooks, so the people who need a page can find it without asking around, and the pages stay in one place as the team grows.

Every notebook belongs to an organization. Owners, admins and members each see and change what their role allows, and invitations let you bring people in with a link that expires after a week.
