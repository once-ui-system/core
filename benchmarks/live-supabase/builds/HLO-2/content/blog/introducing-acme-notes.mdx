---
title: "Introducing Acme Notes"
date: "2026-10-01"
summary: "Acme Notes gives your team one place for notes, and a way to find any of them."
---

Acme Notes is a notes app for teams. Notes live in shared notebooks, so the whole team works from the same pages instead of scattered documents, and a new teammate can find last month's decision in seconds.

Search runs across every notebook you can see, and roles decide who can read, edit and invite. The free plan covers up to three members, and the Team plan adds more members for a per-seat monthly price.
