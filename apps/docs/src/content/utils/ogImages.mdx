When someone shares a link, the network shows the page's Open Graph image. `createOgImageRoute` draws that image for every page: 1200 × 630, dark, with the page's title in your heading font, an eyebrow above it, your domain below, and your mascot beside it, looking at the title. You describe your product once, in a skin, and every page gets its own card.

![A card for a journal post on Fjell, a made-up outdoor brand: the eyebrow "Journal", the title "Packing for a winter trail" with "winter" in an italic serif, and a blue mascot in a hard hat.](/images/docs/og-images/page.jpg)

`Meta.generate` already points a page without its own image at `/api/og/generate?title=…`, so once the route exists, every page has a card. The route needs the **Node.js runtime** (the default) when the skin reads fonts from disk.

## What a card says

The route reads the query string:

| Parameter | What it does |
|---|---|
| `title` | The headline. Words in `*asterisks*` are drawn in the brand color, in `emphasisFont` when the skin has one. |
| `eyebrow` | A short label above the title: the section, a date, a place. `section` works too. |
| `description` | One line under the title. |
| `mood` | The mascot's expression: `happy`, `surprised`, `wink` and the other `Mascot` moods. |
| `layout` | `page` (the default), `hero` for a launch or a home page, or `photo`. |
| `image` | A photo for the card, from your own origin or a host in `imageHosts`. A card with an image uses the `photo` layout. |
| `v` | Ignored by the route. Change it to give a card a new URL, since networks cache images by URL. |

Build the URL with `ogImageUrl`:

## Skin

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | The product's name, drawn as the wordmark and, faintly, behind the mascot. |
| `domain` | Shown at the bottom of every card. |
| `brand`, `accent` | A scheme name (`emerald`, `violet`, …) or a CSS color. The brand lights the mascot and colors the eyebrow and emphasis; the accent adds a smaller glow. |
| `background` | The ground. Near-black by default. |
| `mark` | A small symbol before the name: SVG markup, or an image path or URL. |
| `logo` | A full logo in place of the wordmark. |
| `mascot` | A look, with the same options as `Mascot` and an `accessory` as SVG markup or JSX, or `{ svg }` for your own drawing in a 200 × 200 box. `false` leaves it out. |
| `fonts` | TTF, OTF or WOFF files (not WOFF2), each from `data`, a `path` read from disk, or a `google` family fetched once per server. If a font can't be loaded, the card still draws in the fallback face. |
| `headingFont`, `bodyFont`, `monoFont`, `emphasisFont` | Which family from `fonts` each role uses. |
| `mood` | The mascot's expression when a card doesn't ask for one. |
| `imageHosts` | Hosts `image=` may load from, besides your own origin. |

Fonts from disk are the safest choice: a card renders on the first crawl of a page, and a Google Fonts outage would otherwise draw it in the fallback face.

## Caching

Cards are served with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, so a CDN draws each one once. When you change the skin, change `v` in your URLs.

## mascotSvg

The card draws your mascot with `mascotSvg`, which returns the mascot as a standalone SVG string: the same look options as `Mascot`, one still expression, every color written out. Use it anywhere React can't animate, such as emails and favicons.

Scheme names such as `brand` need a color through `schemes`, since there is no CSS to read them from. Kawaii moods draw their base mood, with blush where they have it.
