`DataTable` is [`Table`](/components/table) in 3.0: the same columns, rows, sorting, search and filters, selection, pagination and column visibility under the short name. The 3.0 codemod renames it (see the migration guide, "Components that merged"), with `DataTableColumn`, `DataTableSort`, `DataTableLabels`, `DataTableColumnFilter`, `DataTableFilters`, `DataTableFilterValue` and `DataTableQuery` as `TableColumn`, `TableSort`, `TableLabels`, `TableColumnFilter`, `TableFilters`, `TableFilterValue` and `TableQuery`.
