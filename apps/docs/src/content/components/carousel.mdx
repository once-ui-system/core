## Auto Play

You can enable auto play with the `play` prop. Each field has its own default: `play={{ auto: true }}` moves every 3000ms with a pause button. Autoplay holds while the pointer is over the carousel, stops when keyboard focus comes into it, and does not start for a visitor who asks for reduced motion; the button then offers Play. Name the button in another language with `pauseLabel` and `playLabel`, and the carousel itself with `aria-label`.

<CodeBlock
  marginTop="16"
  marginBottom="24"
  preview={
