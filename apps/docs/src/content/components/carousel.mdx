## Snap mode

`mode="snap"` swaps the cross-fade for a touch-first track the slides scroll along, with native snapping, drag, arrows and dots. Its `aspectRatio` defaults to `"16 / 9"`, `controls="contained"` draws the dots over the slides, and `translateY`, `defaultRevealed`, `thumbnail` and `play` do not apply. It was `Swiper` in 2.x.

### Interactions

Snap mode uses native CSS scroll-snap, providing smooth touch gestures on mobile, trackpad scrolling, and mouse drag-to-scroll on desktop. Simply click and drag to navigate between slides.

### Controls

The `controls` prop supports three options: `true` (appear on hover), `false` (hidden), or `"contained"` (always visible inside the carousel). Buttons are automatically hidden when at the first or last slide.
