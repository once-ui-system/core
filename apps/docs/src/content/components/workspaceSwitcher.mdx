One switcher for every multi-tenant app. Put it in `SidebarHeader`, or in the top bar on a phone. The trigger shows the current item's mark and name. The list pins the current item on top, groups the rest under headings with counts, shows a search field once there are more than six items, and ends with a create button.

On a phone (at and below `mobileBelow`, default `"s"`) the list opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen: near the thumb, as wide as the screen, so long names wrap instead of being cut. Elsewhere it is a dropdown under the trigger, at least 20rem wide. In a Sidebar folded to its rail, the trigger is the mark alone.

## Behaviour

- **Current pinned:** the current item sits on top under its own heading (`pinCurrent`, default on), marked selected with a check.
- **Groups:** `groups` names the headings and their order; each item names its group by `id`. Items without a known group come last. With no `groups`, the list sits under `labels.title`. `counts` (default on) adds the number of items to each heading.
- **Search:** above `searchAbove` items (default 6; `false` never) a field filters every group by name and `keywords`, with an empty state.
- **Create:** `onCreate` adds a button at the foot that closes the list first. `footer` adds more under it.
- **Loading:** `loading` with no current item shows placeholders the size of the mark and name, so nothing moves when the list arrives.
- **Your own trigger:** `trigger` replaces the default one. On a phone it is given `onClick`, so use a Button or an IconButton.
- **Controlled or not:** `value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange` and `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange`.
- **Words:** `labels` (`title`, `current`, `search`, `noMatch`, `empty`, `loading`, `pick`, `create`) for another language.
