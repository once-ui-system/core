One press of a `ConfirmButton` never acts. In the default `"twice"` mode the
first press arms it: the label asks for a second press, the button turns
`armedTone` (danger by default) and a bar runs down the time left. A second
press within `timeout` runs `onConfirm`. Escape, moving focus away or the time
running out disarms it, and a double click's second press is ignored, so a
double click never confirms by accident.

Reach for it where a dialog would be too slow and one press too easy: a manual
trip, stopping a deploy, deleting a row in a dense table.

## Hold

`mode="hold"` fills the button while it is held, by pointer or by Space or
Enter, and acts when it is full (`holdDuration`, 1.5 s by default). Letting go
early cancels.

## Accessibility

Every change is in words: the label changes as the button arms or fills, and a
status line tells a screen reader how to confirm or cancel. The bar and the
colour only repeat what the label says.
