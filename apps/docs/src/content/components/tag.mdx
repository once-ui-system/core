`Tag` is a compact label with optional icons on either side. It comes in three kinds:

- a **static label** for a status, a category or a piece of metadata;
- an **announcement** (`variant="announce"`), the pill above a hero that links to what is new;
- a **toggle** (`selected` or `onRemove`), a choice in a filter row or a value in `TagInput`.

## Announcement

`variant="announce"` is the pill that announces something: a rounded tag with a shine that sweeps across it now and then. It draws in `brand` unless given a `tone`. This was `Badge` in 2.x.

### Link and arrow

`href` makes any Tag a link, and turns on an `arrow` that slides in on hover. Each tag's arrow follows hover on that tag alone, so a page can hold as many as it needs without giving them ids.

## Toggle

With `selected` or `onRemove` a Tag is a toggle: a `role="button"` with `aria-pressed`, pressed by a click, Enter or Space. An `onClick` alone keeps the static look, in its tone and size, as a plain button. Selected, it draws in its tone (`brand` unless given one); unselected, in neutral. `selected` defaults to false. This was `Chip` in 2.x, where it defaulted to true.

### Removable

`onRemove` adds a remove button beside the tag. Removing never counts as a click on the tag, and `removeButtonProps` reaches the button, for its `aria-label` or icon. A tag with only `onRemove` is a plain label beside its button, not a toggle; `TagInput` renders its values this way, with `selected`.
