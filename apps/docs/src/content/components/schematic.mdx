`Schematic` draws parts and the lines between them from two lists, so a plant,
a network or a pipeline is data, not a drawing. Dashes run along a line in the
direction of its `flow` as dots in the brand colour, at its speed, with an arrow that shows the direction
even when motion is reduced. A pump turns while it runs.

Parts are outlined in the neutral scale and only take a tone when something is
wrong. A part's state reads without colour: `warning` has a dashed outline,
`alarm` a heavy one, `off` is dashed and struck through, and each writes its
state under the part. Parts fill with the page colour; on a panel, set
`--schematic-background` on an ancestor to the panel's colour, such as
`var(--surface-background)`. A screen reader hears every part with its reading and state, and
then the flows.

## Parts

`vessel`, `tank`, `pump`, `valve`, `exchanger`, `turbine`, `sensor` (an
instrument bubble with its `tag`) and `box`. Positions are in the drawing's own
units; the `viewBox` is fitted to the parts unless you give one.

## Selecting

With `onNodeSelect`, every part is a button (Enter or Space presses it), and
`selected` draws a ring round one.

## Phones

Below `minWidth` (560 px by default) the drawing scrolls sideways instead of
shrinking its text out of reach.
