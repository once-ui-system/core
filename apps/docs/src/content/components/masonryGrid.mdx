`MasonryGrid` folded into [`Grid`](/components/grid#masonry) in 3.0: use `<Grid masonry columns={3}>`. The 3.0 codemod rewrites it (see the migration guide, "Components that merged"); `columns`, `gap` and the `l`, `m`, `s` and `xs` overrides mean what they did.
