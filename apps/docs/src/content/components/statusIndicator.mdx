## Pulse

With `pulse`, the dot sits in a ring that keeps pulsing outward, to draw the eye to something live (2.x's `Pulse`). A pulsing dot takes its palette from `tone` (default `"brand"`) rather than `scheme`, and `pulseSize` overrides the ring's size. It is decorative unless you give it an `aria-label`, which makes it an image like the plain dot.
