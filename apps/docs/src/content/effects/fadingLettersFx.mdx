`FadingLettersFx` folded into [`TypeFx`](/effects/typeFx#fade-mode) in 3.0: use `<TypeFx mode="fade" words="…" state="entering">`. The 3.0 codemod rewrites it (see the migration guide, "Components that merged"): `text` became `words`, `animationState` became `state`, and `messageIdx={n}` became `delay={n * 2500}`.
