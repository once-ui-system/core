The `ParticleFx` component fills its area with drifting particles that can push away from or pull toward the pointer. It was called `Particle` before 2.0; that name still works as a deprecated alias until 3.0.

## Shape

Set `shape` to gather part of the field into a mark: `"star"` (four points), `"ring"`, `"diamond"`, or your own points in a -1 to 1 box, such as a logo traced to a few hundred points. `gather` is the share of particles that form it, and `focus` places it as fractions of the area: `x`, `y` and a `radius` of the smaller side.

`sparks` draws that share of the shape's particles larger in `sparkColor`. `glow` puts a soft light in the same colour behind the shape, and `orbits` draws faint rings around it. With `entrance`, the particles start scattered and gather into place the first time the area shows.

## Performance

ParticleFx draws every particle on one canvas, so a field of a thousand stays smooth. It automatically detects low-end devices and the `prefers-reduced-motion` system setting. When either is detected, particles are placed statically without animation. The animation also pauses when the element is outside the viewport. You can override this with the `reducedMotion` prop.

See [useReducedMotion](/hooks/useReducedMotion) for details.
