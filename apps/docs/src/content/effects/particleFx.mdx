The `ParticleFx` component fills its area with drifting particles that can push away from or pull toward the pointer. It was called `Particle` before 2.0; that name still works as a deprecated alias until 3.0.

## Performance

ParticleFx automatically detects low-end devices and the `prefers-reduced-motion` system setting. When either is detected, particles are placed statically without animation. The animation also pauses when the element is outside the viewport. You can override this with the `reducedMotion` prop.

See [useReducedMotion](/hooks/useReducedMotion) for details.
