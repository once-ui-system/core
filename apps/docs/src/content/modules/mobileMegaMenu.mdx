`MobileMegaMenu` folded into [`MegaMenu`](/modules/megaMenu#collapsing) in 3.0: use `<MegaMenu collapseBelow="always">`, or `collapseBelow="m"` for one menu that switches by itself. The 3.0 codemod rewrites it (see the migration guide, "Components that merged"); `menuGroups` and `onClose` mean what they did.
