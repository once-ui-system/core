`Fieldset` groups related fields under a name. It renders a real `<fieldset>` and `<legend>`, so a screen reader announces the section on entering it, and `disabled` turns off every native control inside at once. The fields are laid out by a `Form` grid, so a section takes the same `density`, `columns` and `span`.

  marginBottom="24"

## Sections in a form

Put several sections in one `Form`. Each one fuses its own group of corners, and takes the form's `size` unless it sets its own.

```tsx
<Form as="form" density="spacious" onSubmit={save}>
  <Fieldset legend="Account" density="stacked">
    <Input id="email" placeholder="Email" />
    <PasswordInput id="password" placeholder="Password" />
  </Fieldset>
  <Fieldset legend="Profile" columns={{ xs: 1, m: 2 }}>
    <Field label="First name"><Input id="first" /></Field>
    <Field label="Last name"><Input id="last" /></Field>
  </Fieldset>
  <SubmitButton>Save</SubmitButton>
</Form>
```

## Disabled

`disabled` disables every native control in the section, the way a disabled `<fieldset>` always has. Use it for a section that does not apply yet, such as a shipping address while "same as billing" is checked.
