`ColorButton` is a small secondary button that shows a swatch and the colour's hex, and opens the browser's colour picker. Before a colour is picked it shows its `label` beside a swatch of every hue. Use it for a custom colour beside a row of preset swatches, where a full [ColorInput](/once-ui/form-controls/colorInput) field would be too heavy.

## Preview and commit

- `onValueChange` fires on every drag while the picker is open. Use it for a live preview.
- `onValueCommit` fires once, when the picker closes on a new colour. Save, send or record an undo step here, so a drag does not produce dozens of saves.

The button shows the dragged colour while the picker is open, even before the host commits it.

## Accessibility

The button is the control. Its accessible name is the `label` followed by the colour (`Own colour: #00B5BA`). The native colour field it opens is out of the Tab order and hidden from assistive technology. To link a note under the button, such as a contrast warning, pass its id as `aria-describedby`.
