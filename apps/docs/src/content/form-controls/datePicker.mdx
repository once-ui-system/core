## Localization

Pass `locale`, a BCP 47 tag such as `"de"` or `"pt-BR"`, to show month and weekday names, and the time picker's time and AM/PM, in that language. The names come from the browser's `Intl` API, so no locale data is bundled. Without `locale` they are in English.

The picker's own words come from `labels`: `previousMonth`, `nextMonth`, `backToCalendar`, `hours`, `minutes`, `am` and `pm` are strings, and `month` and `year` are functions that name the month and year buttons from the value they show (`"Month: March, change month"` by default; keep the value in the name, as it is the button's text). Any label left out stays English, and `am` and `pm` default to the locale's words. `DateInput`, `DateRangePicker` and `DateRangeInput` take the same `labels` and pass them to their calendars.

## Range

With `range`, the picker selects a start and an end date, highlighting the days between (2.x's `DateRangePicker`). `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` then take a `DateRange`, `{ startDate, endDate }`: the first pick sets the start alone, the second the end. `months={2}` shows two months side by side (2.x's `dual`), and `minDate` and `maxDate` bound the days that can be picked.

## Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation support for date selection
- Clear visual indicators for selected dates
- Proper ARIA attributes for screen readers
- The month and year buttons say what they do as well as what they show ("Month: March, change month"); `labels` translates them
